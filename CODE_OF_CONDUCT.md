# Code of conduct

Tern follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal
attacks, discriminatory language and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable in issues,
pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer, [@nedimperva](https://github.com/nedimperva),
privately, or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may ban
anyone who keeps breaking it, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
