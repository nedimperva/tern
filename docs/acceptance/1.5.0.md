# 1.5.0 (warm look, sidebar, settings): acceptance checklist

The owner ticks these with a dev build or the release built from this branch; agents never tick them. Mark a
check `- [x] (owner YYYY-MM-DD, Linux/Windows)` when it passed, or leave a note under it when it did not.

## Window and look

- [ ] Tern's own title bar: the sidebar button at the left; minimize, maximize and close at the right; dragging the
      empty bar moves the window and a double click maximizes. Close turns red under the pointer.
- [ ] Light and dark (Settings → Appearance → Theme) both look warm: no cold blue greys anywhere.
- [ ] The focused row in the list has no coloured bar at its left.
- [ ] Moving with j/k, Tab and Esc shows no black outlines; a focused button shows a soft navy ring.

## Sidebar

- [ ] Wide window: the sidebar stays beside the list; Ctrl+\ hides it until shown again.
- [ ] One account: a single Inbox row at the top, no second Inbox under the account. Two accounts: All Inboxes at
      the top and each account's Inbox under it.
- [ ] Each account shows Inbox, Drafts, Sent and Archive; "More · N" opens the rest and "Less" closes them.
- [ ] Clicking an account's name, Labels or Searches folds it; the fold is the same after restarting Tern.
- [ ] With many labels open, only the folders and labels scroll; New mail and Settings stay put.
- [ ] Archive, Sent, Drafts, Junk and Trash show no unread count; Inbox and labels do.

## Inbox Zero and the morning screen

- [ ] An empty inbox shows a warm scene (paper sky, sand, navy tern), in light and dark.
- [ ] The morning screen (first open of a day with something waiting) is warm too, with a terracotta day line.

## Settings

- [ ] The sections list looks like the sidebar, with an icon per section and the account's dot.
- [ ] Every section shows its settings in cards, each control beside its name; dropdowns have a thin chevron.
- [ ] Checkboxes are navy, not the system's green or blue.
- [ ] Settings → Writing style: with AI off it says so and opens Settings → AI; with AI and Write in my style on,
      a card per account with Learn.
- [ ] Settings → AI: the status, providers, Mail it may read, the feature table and what is kept are cards; the
      Writing style row opens that page.

## Release

- [ ] Settings → About says 1.5.0.
- [ ] One draft release with the 1.5.0 notes, and `latest.json` naming Linux and Windows.
