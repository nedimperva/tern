# 1.6.0 (moods, the tide, the companion, folding): acceptance checklist

The owner ticks these with a dev build or the release; agents never tick them. Mark a check
`- [x] (owner YYYY-MM-DD, Linux/Windows)` when it passed, or leave a note under it when it did not.

## Window

- [ ] Linux: no title bar of Tern's own and no window buttons; the sidebar button sits left of the tabs.
- [ ] Windows: Tern's own title bar and its buttons still work.
- [ ] Moods: Dawn, Dusk and Night Sea each look whole; System follows the desktop.
- [ ] Omarchy: with Mood → Omarchy, switching the Omarchy theme recolours Tern within a few seconds.
- [ ] Done slides a row right, delete left, snooze up; the rows below close up; nothing moves with reduced motion.

## The day

- [ ] The tide on the first open of a day: greeting, Needs you, Waiting on others, To read with minutes; a card opens
      its conversation; Enter, Shift E and Esc work.
- [ ] Inbox Zero's Horizon matches the hour and tonight's moon; the tide report shows on Sunday.
- [ ] Wing beat (Settings → Notifications), when on: a soft sound as a mail leaves the outbox, none in quiet hours.

## Reading

- [ ] A conversation reads as a thread: every message one line, only the newest open, at the end.
- [ ] Quotes, Outlook "From: … Sent:" blocks, "--" signatures and company legal notices fold behind ···.
- [ ] Signature logos and icons do not show as attachment cards; real attachments do and open.
- [ ] Separate mails with the same subject (orders, invoices) are separate conversations; replies stay together.
- [ ] Coming back to a conversation with new mail: "Since you last read" and Jump to it.
- [ ] Settings → Who writes most lists senders fast; Unsubscribe opens their page; To Other moves them.
- [ ] A link in a mail (an Unsubscribe link in a newsletter) opens in the default browser; the mail stays.
- [ ] Leaving a conversation opened from search never leaves the window half drawn.

## Companion and writing (AI parts with AI on for the account)

- [ ] Ctrl+. opens the companion: Ask answers with message chips and a receipt; with AI off it sends nothing.
- [ ] Person, Promises (ticks stay) and Files work without AI.
- [ ] A reply of bullet points: Ctrl+J → "Write out these points"; Shorter / Warmer / Firmer show what changed.
