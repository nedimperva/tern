# 1.7.0 (one-column companion, calmer threads): acceptance checklist

The owner ticks these with a dev build or the release; agents never tick them. Mark a check
`- [x] (owner YYYY-MM-DD, Linux/Windows)` when it passed, or leave a note under it when it did not.

## Companion

- [ ] `i`, `Ctrl+.` and the header's panel icon show and hide one column with no tabs.
- [ ] The person on top with "N from them · N from you · you answer in ~…"; × closes it.
- [ ] Day, place and codes in one card with tiles; Copy copies a code; Snooze until then and Open the map work.
- [ ] You owe / Owed to you show only when there is something; ticks stay; a sentence shows its message.
- [ ] "u prilogu šaljem sliku" is not under You owe; "poslaću sutra" is under Owed to you, due tomorrow.
- [ ] Files open; Recent with them lists the person's recent mail.
- [ ] Ask at the foot: a suggestion or Enter asks, the answer shows at the end of the column (AI on); with AI off
      the foot links to Settings → AI and sends nothing.
- [ ] Note in the selection bar opens the companion with the note's field focused.

## Thread

- [ ] A heading for each day; times on the lines; your own closed lines grey with "↳".
- [ ] The newest message is a card; a line opens in place as a card.
- [ ] Under the newest: suggested replies (AI on), Reply to … (R), Reply all, Forward.
- [ ] With the companion open, the line under the subject says "N messages · days", without the people.
- [ ] A signature after "--" or "Srdačan pozdrav" folds to one line naming its sender; a click shows it whole.

## Window

- [ ] Thin scrollbars without arrows; the list, conversation and companion show theirs only under the pointer.
      Linux and Windows.
- [ ] No line above the inbox; `g o` opens the morning screen; Settings → Reading → Start of the day is screen or off.
