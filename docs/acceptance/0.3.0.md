# 0.3.0: acceptance checklist

The owner ticks these against a real account and a real window; agents never tick them. Mark a check
`- [x] (owner YYYY-MM-DD, account)` when it passed, or leave a note under it when it did not. Each line says what
to do and what to look for. Version under test: 0.3.0 (PR #37), the first focused Linux beta (M2) build;
see [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md).

Unless a line says otherwise, check on Linux (Omarchy/Hyprland). Lines marked **Windows** are for the Windows build.

## Reading (package 02, docs/design/ui.md §4.2)

- [ ] Open a long conversation (five or more messages): the first and the last two show, the middle folds into
      "N earlier messages" with small avatars. Clicking it, or `p` into it, unfolds it.
- [ ] The open message is a card close to the background; closed ones are flat lines. Your own messages carry a
      light tint. Replies days apart show a "3 days later" divider.
- [ ] An open message says who it went to ("to you, Tom Berg"), the full date, and how long ago on the right.
- [ ] `n` / `p` move between messages: a closed one gets a grey background, an open one a firmer outline. No blue bar.
- [ ] Scroll down: the big subject scrolls away and a slim bar with the subject and Done fades in. Scroll back to
      the top slowly: nothing flickers.
- [ ] Reply, Reply all and Forward sit after the last message; their keys show on hover. Clicking Reply opens the
      inline reply in their place.
- [ ] `Shift F` hides the list and centres the conversation; `Shift F` again brings the list back.
- [ ] A newsletter with tracking pixels says "Blocked N trackers and M images". After "Load images" it says
      "Images shown · N trackers still blocked".
- [ ] A long mail shows "N min read". A mailing-list mail shows "Mailing list · Unsubscribe"; Unsubscribe opens
      the sender's page in the browser (or compose, for an address).
- [ ] Reading view, then "Show original": the mail is all there, with no empty space below it.
- [ ] A wide designed mail (Apple, a store receipt) fits the pane, slightly smaller, nothing cut off on the right.
- [ ] Settings → Reading → Sender logos → "Show logos": company senders get their logo within a moment of opening;
      Gmail and Outlook senders keep initials. Off again: initials everywhere.
- [ ] Light and dark: no box of a different shade behind a message's text.
